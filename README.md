# FiveLettersOnlyGooglePlayPrivacyPolicy
Five Letters Only on Google Play privacy policy and account removal
Privacy Policy for Five Letters Only

Effective Date: January 31, 2026
1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Shadorma Game Studios ("we," "us," or "our") handles information when you play Five Letters Only. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring a transparent gaming experience.
2. Information We Collect

Because we use Google Play Games Services, we do not host your account or store your password on our own servers.

    Google Play Games Profile: We access your public Gamer ID and display name to show your score on the global leaderboards.

    Game Progress: Your high scores and achievement progress are sent to Google Play Games.

    Device Information: We may collect non-identifying data (OS version, device model) solely for performance optimization and bug fixing.

3. How We Use Your Data

The information we access is used strictly for:

    Displaying your rank on the Five Letters Only Leaderboards.

    Unlocking achievements through the Google Play ecosystem.

    Ensuring the game runs smoothly on your specific device.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Your information is only shared with:

    Google: To provide leaderboard and achievement services.

    Legal Authorities: Only if required by law to comply with a judicial proceeding or court order.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We believe you should have control over your data.

    Automatic Deletion: If you delete your Google Play Games profile, your associated leaderboard scores for Five Letters Only will be removed automatically by Google.

    Manual Deletion Request: You may request that we manually remove your high score from our active leaderboards. To do so, please email us at [Insert Your Email Address]. We will process verified requests within 30 days.
    Optional Manual Deletion: We reserve the right to add or remove an option which allows the player to delete their own high scores from within the game.
    
    Online Management: You can manage or delete your game data at any time via your Google Account Settings.

6. Children's Privacy

Five Letters Only is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with data, please contact us for immediate removal.
